About

Hi, I’m a Game Production graduate from Finland who likes making games and teaching. As an indie game developer, I’ve worked on different games targeting Steam, PS5, Android, and Web. I also have teaching experience at Jamk University of Applied Sciences!

My favourite games of all time are The Witcher 3, Red Dead Redemption 2, Persona 3, Hunt: Showdown, THE FINALS, and Minecraft.
